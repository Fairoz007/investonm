Executive Overview
Shomoukh International Investment is a privately held investment company established in the Sultanate of Oman in 2006 by the Al Hashmi family. The firm operates as both an investment company and a strategic development platform focused on identifying and developing high-value opportunities across strategic sectors.

For nearly two decades, the organization has focused on building and managing investments that contribute to sustainable economic growth and long-term value creation.

Through its diversified portfolio and international partnerships, Shomoukh International Investment connects capital, expertise, and opportunities across Oman and global markets while supporting innovation and economic development.

Headquartered in Muscat, the firm continues to expand its investment activities across emerging industries and international markets.

Oman’s Strategic Advantages
Strategic Location
Oman is strategically positioned at the crossroads of major global trade routes connecting Asia, Africa, and Europe. This geographic advantage enables businesses to access international markets efficiently while benefiting from strong regional connectivity.
Modern Infrastructure
The Sultanate of Oman has developed world-class infrastructure that supports global commerce and economic growth. This includes international airports, deep-water ports, modern highway networks, and expanding digital connectivity.
Business Ecosystem
Oman offers a dynamic business environment supported by industrial zones, free zones, and technology hubs. These platforms provide investors with infrastructure, regulatory incentives, and access to regional and international markets.
Investor-Friendly Legislation
Recent regulatory reforms have strengthened Oman’s investment environment. Investors benefit from simplified licensing procedures, transparent regulatory frameworks, and the ability to hold full foreign ownership in many sectors.
Competitive Operating Costs
Compared with many international markets, Oman offers competitive operating costs while maintaining high-quality infrastructure and strong access to global markets.

Incentives to Invest
The Sultanate of Oman offers a supportive and competitive environment for investors seeking long-term opportunities across strategic sectors. Over the past decade, the country has implemented significant economic reforms and investment policies designed to encourage international investment and private sector growth.
Key advantages for investors include:

•	Competitive Tax Environment – Oman maintains an attractive corporate tax framework with incentives designed to support business development and long-term investment.
•	Access to Free Zones and Special Economic Zones – Investors benefit from well-established economic hubs such as Duqm Special Economic Zone, Sohar Freezone, and Salalah Free Zone. These zones provide world-class infrastructure, investment incentives, and streamlined regulatory procedures.
•	Government Support for Strategic Industries – The government continues to encourage investment in sectors aligned with national economic diversification priorities, including energy, logistics, tourism, manufacturing, technology, and infrastructure.
•	Investor-Friendly Legislation – Modern regulatory reforms, including the Oman Foreign Capital Investment Law, provide enhanced protections for investors, simplified licensing procedures, and allow foreign investors to hold full ownership in many sectors.
•	Strategic Economic Vision – Oman’s national development framework under Oman Vision 2040 aims to diversify the economy, strengthen the private sector, and attract global investment.
These advantages position Oman as a stable and attractive destination for long-term investment and strategic partnerships.

Chairman’s Message

It is within this enduring spirit that Shomoukh International Investment was founded. Our purpose has always been guided by the belief that investment should contribute not only to economic progress, but also to the development of vibrant communities and sustainable opportunities for future generations.

Oman occupies a unique position in the world—geographically, historically, and culturally. Located at the crossroads of global trade routes, the country continues to strengthen its role as a center for cooperation, investment, and responsible development.
Through our investments and strategic partnerships, we seek to support this national journey by developing ventures that foster innovation, strengthen industries, and contribute to long-term prosperity. As we look to the future, our ambition remains clear: to contribute to a world where opportunity is expanded, communities thrive, and Oman continues to stand as a place where cultures, ideas, and markets meet.
